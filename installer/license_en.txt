VitaParms – Licence and disclaimer
==================================

IMPORTANT: VitaParms is a personal diary for health-related readings and NOT a
medical device. It does not make diagnoses and does not replace medical advice.
Use it at your own risk.

The data is stored encrypted. Keep the recovery key in a safe place and make
regular backups. Uninstalling keeps your databases and settings.
